Who We Are

Craftsmanship you can see in every corner.

Jimmalo Styles Construction is a Gauteng-based building and construction outfit. We're best known for concrete designed paving — coloured, patterned driveways, patios and walkways — but our team also handles brick paving, building, plumbing, plastering and painting from start to finish.

Every job is worked by our own hands-on crew, from the first site visit to the final sweep-down. No shortcuts, no disappearing subcontractors — just a team that treats your property like it's their own and a finish we're happy to put our name on.

  • Reliable scheduling — we turn up when we say we will
  • Fair, transparent pricing with no hidden extras
  • Precision finishing, built to handle Gauteng's weather

What We Do

Built around one standard: done properly.

From a single decorative driveway to a full building job, our services cover the work Gauteng homes and businesses need most.

Flagship Service

Concrete Designed Paving

Driveways, patios and walkways in custom colours and patterns — chevron, herringbone, wave, checkerboard, hexagon and feature designs, poured and finished on site.

All Types of Brick Paving

Clay and concrete block pavers, interlockers and cobble styles — laid tight and level for driveways, walkways and outdoor areas.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting with careful prep and a clean, lasting finish — from single rooms to full exteriors.

Plastering

Smooth, durable wall finishes inside and out, prepped properly so paint and coatings last.

Building

New structures, additions and ground-up building work, managed and built by our team from foundation to finish.

Plumbing

Pipe installations, repairs and fittings — from leaks and geysers to full bathroom and kitchen plumbing work.
